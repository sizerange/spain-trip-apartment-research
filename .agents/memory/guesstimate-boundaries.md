---
name: Guesstimate boundaries
description: Policy for replacing useful numeric unknowns without weakening apartment publication or financial trust.
---

Use researched midpoint-and-range guesstimates for numeric gaps when a defensible household or local-market reference exists. Every displayed guessed figure must carry a small visible `guesstimate` label.

Do not use guessed apartment rent, exact-date availability, or unverified categorical amenities to qualify a pair for publication or affordability. Verified and quoted figures remain distinct.

**Why:** The user prefers useful approximate information over zero-like blanks, but affordability and availability claims can create unsafe false confidence.

**How to apply:** Estimate descriptive context such as size, walking distance, and planning costs. Preserve strict importer and affordability validation, and keep exact availability unconfirmed until checked.
