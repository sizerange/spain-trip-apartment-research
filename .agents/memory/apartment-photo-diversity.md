---
name: Apartment photo diversity
description: Project requirement for selecting and ordering apartment listing photos.
---

Apartment cards must not show several angles of the same room or furniture. The first four images should cover distinct subjects and prioritize useful context: one representative interior, balcony or terrace, a view outward from the home, and the building exterior or street when the source provides them.

**Why:** Repeated interior angles are uninformative for comparing apartments. The user explicitly values the relationship between the apartment and its surroundings.

**How to apply:** When importing or curating listing photos, use source photos only, provide descriptive subject-based alt text, reject duplicate URLs, require outdoor context, and include at least three distinct subjects. If an ideal category is unavailable, substitute another genuinely different and decision-relevant room or feature.
